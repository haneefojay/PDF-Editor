The first release of Paperless, a local-first PDF editor for the web and Android. Documents stay on your device: there is no account and no upload. The only exception is the optional AI assistant, which sends page text only after you turn it on and press Send.

## Get it

- **Web:** https://pdf-editor-ten-hazel.vercel.app (tested in Chromium-based browsers such as Chrome and Edge; installable from the browser)
- **Android:** download the APK below and install it on Android 6.0 or later. It is signed with a development key, so Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. It is not on Google Play.

## What's in it

- **Read:** continuous scrolling, outline and links, search, night and sepia themes, read aloud, auto-scroll and editable bookmarks.
- **Edit:** change existing text with its font and reflow whole paragraphs; move, resize or delete images; add text, images, shapes and drawings; find and replace; secure redaction.
- **Scan:** camera scanning with automatic page detection, perspective correction, filters and on-device OCR.
- **Convert:** PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint; Word, Excel and PowerPoint files opened as PDFs. All on the device.
- **Comment, fill and sign:** markup, notes and stamps; form filling and form design; drawn, typed and certificate-backed digital signatures.
- **Pages and tools:** reorder, rotate, crop, insert, split and resize pages; headers, footers, watermarks and Bates numbers; passwords and permissions; compression.
- **Built for long documents and for everyone:** PERFORMANCE_LINE An automated accessibility audit (axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA) finds no violations on desktop or phone, in light or dark theme.

## Known limitations

- Not yet tested on real low-end Android phones; phone performance has only been measured in emulation.
- PowerPoint charts and SmartArt, Word headers, footers and footnotes, and legacy .doc, .xls and .ppt files are not converted.
- Text in scripts the bundled fonts don't cover, such as Arabic or Chinese, is reported as missing rather than added to the PDF.
- The web version needs a connection to load; the Android app works fully offline.
